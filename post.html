Where Beauty Meets Brilliance

By Ahvi's Mom

Wednesday July 3rd

Jewellery Styling Tips

  • Balance Your Jewellery - Pair statement pieces with simpler jewellery to keep your look elegant.
  • Mix Metals - Combine gold, silver, and rose gold for a modern, stylish look.
  • Layer Thoughtfully - Layer necklaces, bracelets, or rings to create stylish look.
  • Match the Occasion - Choose jewellery according to the occasion.
  • Make It Personal - Wear jewellery that reflects your personality.

Jewellery Care

  • Keep It Dry - Remove jewellery before bathing, swimming, or exercising.
  • Store Properly - Keep each piece in a soft pouch or jewellery box to prevent scratches.
  • Avoid Chemicals - Keep jewellery away from perfumes, lotions, and harsh cleaning products.
  • Clean Regularly - Gently clean your jewellery with a soft, dry cloth to maintain its shine.

DIY & Design Inspiration

  • Repurpose Old Jewellery - Turn unused jewellery into new designs or accessories.
  • Experiment with Beads - Combine different colours, shapes, and textures to create unique pieces.
  • Try Minimal Designs - Create simple rings, bracelets, or necklaces for an elegant everyday look.
  • Mix Different Materials - Experiment with beads, charms, chains, pearls, and threads.
  • Find Inspiration in Nature - Use flowers, leaves, shells, and natural colours as inspiration for jewellery designs.
